The Odin Project website is awesome

The Odin Project is completely free and open to everyone. People around the world can learn web development at their own pace, without paying a cent. Its curriculum is open source, so anyone can see how it is made and help improve it. Knowledge is shared rather than sold, which makes it fair for everyone. It is an investment in people, and many of them give back by helping others and contributing to the web.
